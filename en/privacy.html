Privacy policy

1. Controller

Ali Seckin, Karmarschgasse 41, 1100 Wien, Austria. Email: support@itqan.at

2. In short

  • Itqān works without an account. Your progress and settings then stay on your device.
  • There are no ads, no advertising networks and no user tracking, neither in the app nor on this website.
  • Only if you create the optional account do we store your email address and your progress on a server in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.
  • Only if you expressly agree to the voluntary learning log for research does the app send details of your exercises, without your name and under a random number, to the same server.
  • Only if you yourself tap “Send” after an error does the app send a technical error report, without profile data, to the same server.

3. This website

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., a Microsoft company, USA). When you visit, the host processes your IP address and access data (time, requested file, browser) as technically necessary. We use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising tools, and we load no fonts from third-party servers. Your choice between light and dark appearance is stored locally in your browser (Local Storage) and is not transmitted. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The domain is managed with Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare only resolves the website’s name (DNS); your page requests do not pass through Cloudflare.

4. The app without an account

Progress, weak spots, settings and backups are stored on your device. They are not sent to us. If you use the app in a browser, it is loaded from GitHub Pages (IP address, see section 3). “Back up profile” creates a file that you store or share yourself. The app loads translations of the āyāt and page fonts on first use as files from the same address (GitHub Pages) and then keeps them on your device. If you record yourself while practising, the browser asks for the microphone. The recording is held only in your device’s working memory, is not transmitted and is not stored permanently. It is discarded as soon as you rate the āya or start a new recording.

The app from the App Store and Google Play contains all pages and data and does not download anything while you practise. Your device schedules the daily reminder itself, nothing is transmitted for it. Itqān uses the microphone only when you record your recitation. The recording stays on your device and is not transmitted. When you download and update the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process data under their own privacy policies. We receive no data from them that identifies you personally.

Voluntary learning log for research. Only if you expressly agree in the settings does the app send one line for each exercise to our server (Supabase, Frankfurt region). Each line contains: a random number that is generated on your device and stays the same for all lines from this device, the days since your consent, the number of the section and the chosen division (into sections or pages), the interval in days since the last exercise of this section, the stability estimated by the learning model and the predicted certainty before the exercise, the number of verses in the section, the number of verses with a mistake, with hesitation and with help, the type of exercise, the daily amount and the version of the learning model. Name, email address, recordings, calendar date and location are not sent.

These lines are pseudonymous, not anonymous: through the random number all lines from one device belong together and over time form a learning history. The number is not linked to any name or account, and the request is sent without sign-in. The table holds neither an IP address nor a timestamp, only a consecutive row number. As the provider of the server, however, Supabase processes your IP address and the time of each request in its logs as technically necessary.

Purpose: to examine and improve the learning model scientifically. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in the settings, and all lines sent under your number are then deleted. The number exists only on your device. If it is lost before you withdraw (app deleted, browser data cleared), we can no longer attribute your lines to you and therefore cannot delete them selectively. The data is not passed on to third parties.

Voluntary error report. If an error occurs in the app, it shows a calm banner with “Report error”. Only when you tap “Send” in the dialog does the app send one line to our server (Supabase, Frankfurt region), never automatically. Beforehand the dialog shows exactly what will be sent: the app version, whether you use the app in the browser, installed or from the store, the browser family with its major version, the operating system roughly, the screen size, the name of the view (without names or search terms), the place where the error occurred, the error text and a shortened call stack (addresses reduced to file names, email addresses and long identifiers masked), the day number (days since 1970, not a date with a time), your comment if you write one, and two random numbers: one for this report so that nothing arrives twice, and one for this device that serves only to limit the number of reports per device and day. This number is not linked to any account, any profile or any learning log. Profile content, names, email address, recordings and location are not sent. Please do not write names or contact details in the comment field. If the report cannot be delivered (for example without a connection), it stays on your device and is sent again later. You can also copy it or send it by email. The table holds neither an IP address nor a timestamp. As the provider of the server, however, Supabase processes your IP address and the time of each request in its logs as technically necessary.

Purpose: to find and fix errors. Legal basis: your consent by tapping “Send” (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Because the report is not linked to any person, we cannot attribute it to a person and therefore cannot delete it selectively. We delete it as soon as we no longer need it to fix the error. The data is not passed on to third parties.

5. The optional account

Data: Your email address and your progress. Your progress comprises your profile with the name you entered in the app, your settings, the sections you practised with results and weak spots, and the log of your practice. Each log entry carries the time, for exercises also the duration, and a random identifier of the device on which it was created. For sign-in, Supabase also keeps session details (for example sign-in times) and processes your IP address, the time and details of your browser in its logs with each request as technically necessary.

Purpose: Syncing your progress between your devices and backup.

Legal basis: Performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Processors: The data is stored with Supabase (Supabase Inc.) in the Frankfurt am Main region (EU). We send sign-in codes by email through Resend (Resend, Inc., USA, sent via the EU region Ireland). For transfers to the USA the providers rely on the EU Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Duration: Until you delete your account. In the app you will find “Delete account”. It removes your account with the email address, your progress and the log of your practice from our database. Not covered are: your progress on the device (it stays), lines of the voluntary learning log (they are not connected to any account; you delete them by withdrawing consent in the settings), the delivery logs for sign-in codes at Resend (they contain your email address), and technical logs and, where they exist, backup copies at Supabase. The providers delete these according to their own retention periods. “Export my data” gives you a copy beforehand.

6. Support and feedback

If you write to support@itqan.at, we process your message and address to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare Email Routing forwards the address to a mailbox at Google (Gmail). Messages are deleted once they are no longer needed. If you use “Send feedback” in the app, an email to this address opens. If you leave the box ticked it contains technical details such as app version, device, browser and the last error. You send the email yourself.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to support@itqan.at. You can also complain to the supervisory authority: Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40 to 42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at.

8. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will adapt this policy. The current version is always on this page.
