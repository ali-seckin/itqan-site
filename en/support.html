Support

Help with Itqān: contact and answers to the most common questions.

Write to support@itqan.at. We usually reply within a few days. Itqān is a free learning service run by a private individual, so it can sometimes take a little longer.

How to reach us

Email: support@itqan.at

It helps us to know what you did and what happened, your device (iPhone, Android or browser) and the version of Itqān. You find the version number in the profile. Please tell us no more about yourself than your question needs.

Common questions

The sign-in code does not arrive.

First look in your spam folder and check that you typed your address correctly. Then wait about a minute and request a new code, because the server does not send several codes to the same address in quick succession. A code is valid only briefly and only once, so always use the newest one. Itqān has no sign-in with a password: each time you receive a code by email. If nothing arrives even then, write to us with the address you tried to sign in with.

Do I need an account?

No. Every feature of Itqān works without an account. Your progress then stays on your device. The account is optional and serves to sync your progress between your devices and to back it up.

How do I back up my progress and export my data?

Without an account: under Profile › Backup, “Back up profile” creates a file that you keep yourself, for example in Files, in a cloud or in a message to yourself. With an account: under Profile › Backup › Account and sync, “Export my data” gives you all the data of your account as a file.

I am changing devices. How do I take my progress with me?

With an account you sign in on the new device with a code and your progress is there. Without an account you back up your profile on the old device, bring the file to the new one and choose “Load profile” under Profile › Backup. The same applies if you move from the browser to the installed app or to the app from the store, because each of these versions has its own storage.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Profile › Backup › Account and sync › “Delete account”, or by email to support@itqan.at. All steps, and what is deleted and what is not, are on the page Delete account.

How do I change the language?

In Settings (the gear in the profile) under “Language”. Itqān is available in German, English, Turkish, French, Spanish, Arabic and Urdu. You choose the language of the āya translation separately, also in Settings.

Does Itqān work without internet?

Yes. The app from the App Store and Google Play contains all pages. In the browser and as an installed web app, the app itself and every page you have opened once stay on the device. In Settings under “Offline” you can save all 604 pages at once, ideally on Wi-Fi. Only syncing with an account needs a connection. Without a network it stays pending and continues later.

I found an error. How do I report it?

When something goes wrong in the app, “Report error” appears. The dialog shows exactly what would be sent, and nothing is sent until you tap “Send”. Neither your name nor your email address nor your profile goes with it. You can also write to us: under Profile › Help and guides, “Send feedback” opens an email to support@itqan.at, or you write yourself and mention the version of Itqān, your device and what you did last.
