Delete your account and data

Itqān, the app for regularly reviewing the Qurʾān (iPhone, Android and browser)

You can delete your account yourself in the app, and it happens immediately. Or you can request deletion by email to support@itqan.at. If you use Itqān without an account, there is no account of yours with us to delete: your progress then lives only on your own device.

Way 1: In the app, immediately

  1. Open Itqān and go to Profile › Backup › “Account and sync”. If you are not signed in on this device, first sign in with the code we send you by email.
  2. Tap “Delete account” and confirm.
  3. If your sign-in was more than ten minutes ago, Itqān sends you a new code for safety. Enter it and the account is deleted.

If you want a copy beforehand, choose “Export my data” on the same page. Deletion is final and cannot be undone.

Way 2: Without the app, by email

Write from the email address of your account to support@itqan.at with the subject “Delete account”. We usually process the request within 30 days. If there is doubt that the address belongs to you, we will ask you before we delete anything.

What is deleted

  • your account and your email address,
  • your progress on the server: your profile with the name you entered in the app, your settings, and the sections you practised with results and weak spots,
  • the log of your practice on the server.

All of this is stored on our server in Frankfurt am Main (Supabase) and is removed from the database when the account is deleted.

What stays

  • Your progress on the device. It stays until you delete it there: in Settings under “Start over”, or by removing the app or clearing the browser data of the site.
  • Technical traces at our service providers. These include the delivery logs for sign-in codes at Resend (they contain your email address) as well as technical logs and, where they exist, backup copies at Supabase. The providers delete these according to their own retention periods.
  • Your emails to us. If you wrote to support, the correspondence stays until we no longer need it. If you want it deleted, say so in your message.

The voluntary learning log for research

The learning log is not tied to your account but to a random number that exists only on your device. That is why “Delete account” does not delete the learning log. You delete it yourself: in Settings under “Research, voluntary”, tap “Don’t share”. This withdraws your consent, and all lines sent under your number are deleted. If you have no connection at that moment, the app remembers the withdrawal and completes the deletion later. The same happens when you delete your profile in Settings under “Start over” or load a different profile.

To be honest about it: because the number is linked to no name and no email address, we cannot look up the lines for you and cannot delete them on request by email. Only the app knows the number. If you remove the app or clear the browser data before you have withdrawn, nobody knows the number any more. The lines can then no longer be attributed to you and remain without a link to any person. If you do not want that, withdraw first and remove the app afterwards.

Error reports you sent

An error report reaches us only when you yourself tap “Send” in the “Report error” dialog. It contains no name, no email address and no link to your account, your profile or your learning log. Only a free comment that you write yourself can contain text, so please do not write any names in it. Because we cannot attribute a report to a person, we cannot delete it selectively. We delete it as soon as we no longer need it to fix the error.

Further rights

Access, rectification, restriction, data portability and objection are described in the privacy policy. If you have questions, write to support@itqan.at. Help with the app is on the Support page.
